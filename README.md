# AMC 8 — Learn by Playing

**American Mathematics Competition 8**: an interactive, animated solution guide to AMC 8 problems (2026, 2025 and 2024 so far; 2023 and 2022 coming), built for primary-school students.

**Play it here:** https://chun1333.github.io/amc8-2026-learn-by-playing/ (or open `index.html` in any browser; it is one self-contained page and needs nothing installed).

## How each problem works
1. **Problem**: maths is typeset in LaTeX (KaTeX). Pick an answer and see right or wrong straight away.
2. **Hint #1 → #4**: each hint opens with its own picture that kids drag, slide or tap, plus a quick check question. The hints build up the way primary maths-olympiad coaching does: the maths fact you need → a problem-solving tool (bar model, systematic list, pairing, working backwards…) → the key "aha" for this problem → carrying it out. Kids just see "Hint #1, #2…".
3. **Solution**: step by step. Press **Next ▶** and the picture acts out each step, pausing on a question before showing the answer.
4. **Twin Challenge**: same idea, new numbers, for ★★★ mastery.

Geometry pictures use **JSXGraph** (interactive geometry, bundled into the page); the counting and number pictures are custom animated SVG (number lines, fraction bars, bar models, tree diagrams, spinners, base-ten blocks, dice tables…).

## Gallery and domains
Problems are shown as a gallery, colour-coded by maths domain:
ARI Arithmetic & Number Sense · ALG Algebra, Patterns & Sequences · NT Number Theory · GEO Geometry & Spatial Reasoning ·
MEA Measurement & Quantitative Modelling · COM Combinatorics & Discrete Counting · DAT Probability, Data & Sets · LOG Logic, Games & Proof.
You can filter the gallery by domain.

## Game layer
XP and levels with a buddy that grows (🥚 → 🐉), 3-star mastery, **medals in five tiers** (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond), combo streaks, growth-mindset feedback, spaced "memory checks" on expanding intervals (1 → 3 → 7 → 14 → 30 days), and gentle brain-break nudges. No loot boxes and no punishing streaks. Progress is saved in the browser (localStorage).
